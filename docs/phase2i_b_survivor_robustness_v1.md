## Method

For each survivor at its best (63d) horizon the analyzer reuses the Phase 2I-A
panel and the same per-date cross-sectional **Spearman rank IC** (Pearson on
within-date ranks; the GCP venv has no scipy). Everything below is derived from
that one per-date IC series and the same vectorized quintile/spread helpers, so no
nested per-date Python loop is introduced.

### 1. Stability

- per-year and per-quarter rank IC, with sign-match against the overall sign;
- a **rolling 6-month** (126-session) IC view — min/max/mean and the fraction of
  windows whose sign matches the overall sign — when the sample spans at least one
  window;
- IC **information ratio by year** (mean / std of that year's per-date IC);
- **top-minus-bottom quintile spread** and **top-decile hit rate by year**;
- the number of dates and rows per split.

### 2. Regime buckets

- **SPY forward 63d return** positive vs negative — does the edge survive in down
  markets, or only in the bull run? A sign flip across this split is recorded as
  `sign_flips_across_regime` and surfaced as `regime_dependent`;
- **market volatility** high vs low — a median split on SPY's `spy_realized_vol_21d`;
- **feature cross-sectional dispersion** high vs low — a median split on the
  per-date dispersion of the feature itself (its practical "high vs low feature
  volatility regime").

### 3. Robustness

- a **moving-block bootstrap** 95% CI for the mean rank IC, with **block length =
  the 63d horizon** so the heavily overlapping (autocorrelated) forward windows are
  resampled in contiguous blocks rather than as if independent (1,000 resamples,
  seeded);
- a **sign-flip permutation** null for IC significance (1,000 permutations, seeded)
  — a two-sided p for H0: the per-date IC is centered at zero;
- **leave-one-year-out** stability — recompute the mean IC dropping each year and
  check the sign holds;
- a **leave-one-ticker-out** check — drop the single dominant long-leg name and
  re-measure the mean IC.

### 4. Concentration

Per-date top-quintile membership is tallied per ticker to expose whether the long
leg is carried by a few names: the top-3 long-leg share, the unique-ticker count,
and the top contributors (share, dates in top quintile, mean forward excess). A
top-3 share above `0.50` sets `overly_concentrated`.

### 5. Recommendation (per survivor)

```
NEED_MORE_DATA  if n_dates / horizon < min_effective_obs (8)
DROP            else if the bootstrap mean-IC CI includes zero
                     OR the sign-flip permutation null is not rejected
                     OR the IC sign is unstable leaving any single year out
KEEP_AS_RISK_FILTER_ONLY  else if overly concentrated OR the feature is a
                               risk/exposure measure (vol / downside / beta / corr)
KEEP_FOR_MODEL  else
```

The 63d horizon overlaps forward windows ~63-deep, so the **effective** number of
independent observations is roughly `n_dates / 63` — far below the raw date count.
That is why thin survivors (e.g. `momentum_12_1`, which needs ~12 months of
trailing history and so loses 2023) can land in `NEED_MORE_DATA` even with hundreds
of raw dates.

### 6. Research interpretation

The roll-up classifies each survivor's edge as a **volatility / risk premium**
(`realized_vol_21d`, `realized_vol_63d`, `downside_vol_21d`), **beta exposure**
(`rolling_beta_63d`), a **correlation regime** (`rolling_corr_spy_63d`), or
**momentum** (`momentum_12_1`); reports which survivors are regime-dependent or
overly concentrated; states that the set **supports a long-horizon model** (all
edge is at 63d, none at 5/10/21d) and **not** a short-horizon one; and sets
`robust_enough_for_phase2j` conservatively — true only if at least one feature is
genuinely `KEEP_FOR_MODEL` and fewer than half the set is dropped or data-starved.

## How to read the result

Five of the six survivors are risk/exposure measures with a positive sign across a
2023–2026 bull run. The honest prior is that this is a **risk-on / high-beta tilt**
that would invert in a drawdown, so even a statistically robust survivor is at best
a `KEEP_AS_RISK_FILTER_ONLY`, not model alpha. The SPY-forward-return regime split
is the key tell: if the IC flips sign in down markets, the "edge" is a risk premium,
not a market-neutral signal. The bootstrap CI and permutation p, read against the
small `effective_obs`, are the guard against reading overlapping-window noise as
significance. The concentration block guards against a handful of names carrying the
whole long leg.

## Conclusion

Phase 2I-A established that the only measurable signal in this universe is a 63d
volatility / beta / correlation tilt. Phase 2I-B exists to keep us honest before we
build on it: it stress-tests each survivor across years, quarters, rolling windows,
market regimes, an overlap-aware bootstrap, a permutation null, and ticker
concentration, and assigns each a `KEEP_FOR_MODEL` / `KEEP_AS_RISK_FILTER_ONLY` /
`DROP` / `NEED_MORE_DATA` verdict. The output is the evidence base for deciding
whether Phase 2J should design **long-horizon** model candidates — and it asserts no
**production edge**.
