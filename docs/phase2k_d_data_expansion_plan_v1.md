## Why Phase 2K-D follows Phase 2L-B

Phase 2L-B implemented the Phase 2L-A model-free, rank-only walk-forward validation for
the sole Phase 2K-C survivor, `avg_dollar_volume_21d`, at its 63-session horizon. The
validation did **not** pass on the current ~40-name, single-regime 2023–2026 export.
Phase 2L-B trained nothing, created no model candidate, and routed here precisely so
that the response is *more disciplined data*, not a model fit to a thin sample.

On the artifact this plan was generated against, Phase 2L-B returned **`NEED_MORE_DATA`**:
the labeled session calendar yielded only 7 non-overlapping 63-session validation
blocks / **7.0 effective validation observations** after the 63-session embargo, below
the inherited floor of **8.0**, and four gates (majority-sign, bootstrap-CI-excludes-
zero, no-single-window-drives-result, enough-effective-observations) did not pass. The
verdict, the exact gate outcomes, and the reason string are read live from the Phase
2L-B JSON — this document and the plan JSON reflect whatever that artifact actually
says, and the plan handles `WALK_FORWARD_FAIL` symmetrically (listing the failed gates
and their interpretation) if a future run produces that verdict instead.

If Phase 2L-B had **passed**, a data-expansion plan would be the wrong next step
(Phase 2L-C — simple residual signal model candidate design — would be), so the
analyzer refuses to emit a 2K-D plan in that case and says so.

## Was Phase 2L-B `NEED_MORE_DATA` or `WALK_FORWARD_FAIL`?

The `data_gap_analysis` block branches on the live verdict:

- **`NEED_MORE_DATA`** → it records the fold count, the effective validation
  observations, the required floor, and the effective-observation shortfall, and
  explains that with `validation == horizon` each non-overlapping block contributes only
  ~1 effective observation, so clearing the floor needs materially more labeled history
  after the embargo than the current export provides.
- **`WALK_FORWARD_FAIL`** → it lists each failed gate with its observed value,
  threshold, and a plain-English interpretation of what failing that gate means
  out-of-sample.

In both cases the conclusion is the same: the current panel is one extended bull regime,
roughly three years long and only ~40 mega-cap names, which is too short and too narrow
to either accumulate enough independent validation windows or to demonstrate that the
signal holds across regimes.

## Why no model candidate is allowed

The Phase 2K-A model-candidate gate stays locked. No model candidate may be created
until a feature family passes the IC sweep and the out-of-sample robustness /
walk-forward battery on real data spanning more than one market regime, with no
single-regime artifact, no excessive ticker concentration, and no look-ahead leakage.
Phase 2L-B did not clear that bar, so Phase 2K-D sets `continue_model_research_now =
false` and `create_model_candidate_now = false`. This phase trains nothing, fits
nothing, and creates no model candidate. It asserts no **production edge**.

## What data is missing

The `expanded_dataset_requirements` block (seeded from the Phase 2K-A data
requirements) defines what must be acquired before any retest. The items required to
retest the price/volume liquidity candidate `avg_dollar_volume_21d` are:

- **Longer price history** — daily adjusted OHLCV reaching well before 2023, ideally a
  full market cycle including a sustained drawdown.
- **Broader equity universe** — materially more than ~40 mega-caps (toward the full
  liquid S&P 500) to widen each cross-section and reduce long-leg concentration.
- **Point-in-time universe membership** — as-of-date constituent history, never today's
  membership back-applied to the past.
- **Survivorship-bias controls** — coverage of delisted / merged / renamed names over
  their live span.
- **Liquidity / volume history** and **benchmark / factor data** — the direct feature
  input and the residualization controls over the longer span (mostly already on hand).

Point-in-time **fundamentals** and **analyst estimates / earnings surprise** are marked
**future-phase**: they are not required to retest a liquidity feature and only later,
different hypotheses would need them. Sector / industry classification is recommended
(for neutralization and concentration control) but not required for this candidate.

## How much more data is needed

The `target_validation_capacity` block separates the arithmetic floor from the binding
regime constraint:

- **Effective-observation floor** — at least the inherited `min_effective_obs`
  (8.0) effectively independent validation observations after the embargo, i.e. enough
  non-overlapping 63-session validation blocks. The plan computes the implied minimum
  labeled-session and approximate-year counts (`min_train + embargo + folds × validation`,
  plus a horizon tail), and recommends roughly twice the floor for margin.
- **The binding constraint is regime coverage, not block-counting.** The plan therefore
  targets **8–10 years** of daily adjusted OHLCV and **≥ 100 tickers per date** so the
  walk-forward spans market-up, market-down, high-volatility, and low-volatility
  periods, including **at least one sustained drawdown**. A larger block count on a
  single bull regime would still not be a valid out-of-sample test.

If true point-in-time membership cannot be sourced, the minimum-viable dataset must
carry an explicit survivorship caveat and the retest must report its results as
survivorship-biased rather than treating them as clean.

## Data quality and leakage controls

Before any retest, the assembled dataset must pass the `data_quality_gates`: adjusted-
close consistency across the longer span; split / dividend adjustment sanity with no
look-ahead; explicit missing-date handling (no silent forward-fill across non-trading
days); ticker-history continuity; delisting / survivorship treatment; duplicate
`(date, ticker)` detection; **no forward-filled target labels**; and **no look-ahead
universe membership**. These are gates on the data itself, independent of any signal.

## Implementation options

The `implementation_options` block scopes three paths, with cost, point-in-time
membership, pros, and risks for each:

1. **Extend the current free / local export** — fastest and free, but no point-in-time
   membership (survivorship bias) and limited depth / delisted coverage. Recommended to
   attempt first.
2. **Broader free / low-cost sources** — wider universe and longer depth at low spend;
   point-in-time constituent lists remain the scarce piece and must be validated against
   the data-quality gates.
3. **Paid point-in-time dataset** — the soundest basis (clean membership and
   survivorship), but a cost / lead-time decision explicitly **deferred** until the
   feasibility check.

## What Phase 2K-E should verify

`recommended_next_phase` routes to **2K-E — Expanded Historical Universe Data
Feasibility Check**: before retesting any residual signal, verify whether the required
expanded dataset (8–10 years of daily adjusted OHLCV across a materially broader,
point-in-time universe with survivorship coverage) can actually be assembled locally /
free / low-cost. Phase 2K-E scopes source depth, universe breadth, point-in-time
membership availability, and the data-quality gates, and decides whether a paid
point-in-time dataset is warranted. It acquires no paid data, trains no model, and
creates no model candidate.

The `research_retest_plan` then fixes the order once the data exists: re-run Phase
2K-B → 2K-C → 2L-A / 2L-B on the expanded multi-regime panel, and only if the
model-free baseline passes walk-forward there may a later, separately gated phase design
a simple single-feature candidate. The model-v2 serving flag stays disabled until then.
