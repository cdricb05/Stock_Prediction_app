## Why Phase 2J-A forces a research reset

Phase 2I-A surfaced six KEEP features, but all six were strongest only at the 63d
horizon and all bullish — a volatility / beta / correlation tilt, not
stock-picking alpha. Phase 2I-B stress-tested those six survivors with an
overlap-aware moving-block bootstrap, a sign-flip permutation null,
leave-one-year-out and leave-one-ticker-out checks, market- and dispersion-regime
splits, and ticker-concentration analysis — and dropped every one
(`KEEP_FOR_MODEL = 0`, `DROP = 6`, `robust_enough_for_phase2j = false`). Phase
2J-A formalized that into a locked `NO_GO`: `promote_model_v2 = false`,
`build_phase2j_model_candidate = false`.

The honest consequence is that we have **no validated signal to model**. Iterating
on the same six features, or fitting a model to them anyway, would only relabel a
regime-dependent risk premium as alpha. So the disciplined move is a research
reset: define **new** hypotheses with different return drivers, and the data each
one needs, *before* writing any more feature code — and certainly before any model
candidate. This artifact is that reset, captured as a ranked backlog rather than a
narrative, so the next phase has a concrete, cheapest-first starting point.
`upstream_decision_summary.no_go_confirmed` is derived from the upstream artifact
(NO_GO with both promotion flags false), not re-decided here.

## Why we keep the platform but reject the current signal

The signal failed; the research platform did not. The offline, vectorized harness
(2G real-data export → 2I-A IC sweep → 2I-B robustness) ran in seconds, produced
auditable JSON at every step, and is exactly what *caught* the failure. Every
phase emitted machine-readable safety flags, the model-v2 serving flag never
moved, and Paper Trader (preview-only, manual review) stayed fully separated from
this prediction research. We keep all of that. What we reject is the **63d
vol/beta/correlation tilt** — a risk premium that would invert in a drawdown — as
a basis for a model. Keeping the platform and rejecting the signal is the whole
point of the reset.

## The hypothesis backlog

Eight hypothesis families, each with a thesis, the failed-2I signal it replaces,
required data, a minimum-viable test, expected horizon/direction, leakage risks, a
validation gate, and availability / complexity / priority. Families testable on
the **existing 2G export** are flagged `go_to_test = true` and ranked first;
families needing new data are `false` until their data requirement is met.

| # | Family | Testable now? | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `beta_vol_neutralized_residual_signal` | yes | Residualize forward return vs beta & vol, re-run the IC sweep on the residual. |
| 2 | `cross_sectional_mean_reversion` | yes | Beta-neutral short-horizon reversal, kept distinct from the failed raw-return momentum. |
| 3 | `fundamental_quality_value` | no | Quality/value factors driven by fundamentals, not market beta. |
| 4 | `estimate_revisions_and_surprise` | no | Analyst revision momentum & surprise as an information-diffusion signal. |
| 5 | `earnings_event_drift` | no | Post-earnings-announcement drift in event time. |
| 6 | `liquidity_breadth_and_microstructure` | yes | Amihud illiquidity / turnover / breadth, size-neutralized. |
| 7 | `sector_relative_strength` | yes | Strength relative to a name's own sector, removing the market move. |
| 8 | `regime_conditional_signals` | yes | Treat regime as an explicit conditioning variable rather than averaging across it. |

The recommended research sequence starts with the cheapest current-data tests
(the residual signal, then sector-relative / residual mean-reversion / liquidity /
regime tests), then longer-history & broader-universe acquisition, then
point-in-time fundamentals / revisions, then event-based earnings drift, and only
**last** — if a family passes 2I-A IC + 2I-B robustness — scoping a model
candidate.

## Data requirements and leakage risks

Each data requirement records what it is for, whether it must be point-in-time,
its dominant look-ahead risk, low-cost sources to investigate, and blockers:

- **`longer_price_history`** — a full cycle including a sustained drawdown so a 63d
  signal can be tested out of a single bull regime. Low look-ahead, but
  corporate-action adjustment must use only as-of-date data, and delisted names
  must be retained to avoid survivorship bias.
- **`broader_universe`** — beyond the current ~40 mega-caps to cut long-leg
  concentration. **Must be point-in-time**: today's index membership applied to
  history is survivorship bias.
- **`point_in_time_fundamentals`** — as-first-reported financials with correct
  report-date lags. **Highest** leakage risk: restated figures and report-date
  misalignment are the classic factor-research trap.
- **`analyst_estimates`** — consensus and its *revision history* with as-of
  timestamps; using the final/current consensus for a past date imports the future.
- **`earnings_calendar_and_surprise`** — *actual* announcement dates and realized
  surprise; estimated dates and post-hoc surprise revisions both leak.
- **`sector_industry_mapping`** — ideally point-in-time classification; the current
  snapshot is a usable starting approximation.
- **`liquidity_and_volume_history`** — mostly already in the 2G export; features
  must be trailing-only and neutralized for size/price level.
- **`benchmark_and_factor_data`** — SPY/factor series for beta estimation; betas
  and loadings must be trailing-only.

The cross-cutting rule is that **every feature and label must be constructed from
data available as of its date** — point-in-time fundamentals, as-of estimates,
actual (not estimated) earnings dates, and trailing-only betas, vols, and volume
features. The cheap first tests (families 1, 2, 6, 7, 8) deliberately use only the
already-validated 2G real-data export so we learn quickly before spending on data.

## The model-candidate gate

`model_candidate_gate.may_create_model_candidate = false`. **No model candidate may
be created** until a feature family clears every one of these, in order:

1. passes the 2I-A IC sweep above the |IC| floor;
2. passes the 2I-B out-of-sample robustness battery;
3. shows no single-regime artifact (sign-stable across market regimes);
4. shows no excessive ticker concentration in the long leg;
5. has no look-ahead leakage in features or labels;
6. passes out-of-sample validation spanning more than one market regime.

Until all six hold, the model-v2 serving flag stays disabled and no candidate is
scoped — the same discipline that produced the Phase 2J-A NO-GO. No **production
edge** is claimed by this phase.
