## The decision: NO_GO

`decision.go_no_go = "NO_GO"`, `promote_model_v2 = false`,
`build_phase2j_model_candidate = false`. The decision is **derived from the
evidence**, not hardcoded: a GO would require at least one Phase 2I-B
`KEEP_FOR_MODEL` survivor *and* its `robust_enough_for_phase2j` gate to be true.
Neither holds.

### Why Phase 2I-B is a NO-GO

Phase 2I-A surfaced six KEEP features, but all six were strongest only at the 63d
horizon and all bullish — a volatility / beta / correlation tilt, not
stock-picking alpha. Phase 2I-B then stress-tested those six survivors with an
overlap-aware moving-block bootstrap (block length = the 63d horizon), a sign-flip
permutation null, leave-one-year-out and leave-one-ticker-out checks, market- and
dispersion-regime splits, and ticker-concentration analysis. The verdict was
unanimous:

| Recommendation | Count |
|---|---|
| KEEP_FOR_MODEL | 0 |
| KEEP_AS_RISK_FILTER_ONLY | 0 |
| DROP | 6 |
| NEED_MORE_DATA | 0 |

`robust_enough_for_phase2j = false`; dominant edge character =
`volatility_risk_premium`. Every survivor dropped. There is simply nothing that
passed out-of-sample robustness to build a model on.

### Why we must not build a model from the failed 63d risk/volatility signal

The only measurable signal in this universe was a **63d volatility / beta /
correlation tilt**, positive across a 2023–2026 bull run. That is a
regime-dependent **risk premium** — high-beta, high-vol names outperforming while
the market rises — and it is exactly the kind of exposure that **inverts in a
drawdown**. Phase 2I-B confirmed it does not survive the overlap-aware bootstrap,
the permutation null, or leave-one-year-out sign stability. Fitting a Phase 2J
model to it would be fitting to a small-sample (heavily overlapping 63d windows),
single-regime artifact and mislabeling a risk premium as alpha. No **production
edge** exists here.

### Why model-v2 must stay disabled

`PREDICTOR_USE_MODEL_V2` has been off through every phase and must remain off. A
model trained on a dropped signal would, if served, simply tilt the book toward
high-beta names — taking uncompensated regime risk while presenting it as a
prediction edge. The feature flag is the control that keeps a failed research
signal from leaking into serving; it stays disabled until a feature family passes
out-of-sample robustness on real data spanning more than one market regime.

## What still worked (keep the platform, not the signal)

The signal failed; the research platform did not. Keep:

- **Local research harness** — the offline, vectorized pipeline (2G real-data
  export → 2I-A IC sweep → 2I-B robustness) ran in seconds and produced auditable
  JSON at every step.
- **Safety flags** — every phase emitted machine-readable flags (no DB write, no
  migration, no deployment, model-v2 off, no **production edge**).
- **Feature-flag discipline** — `PREDICTOR_USE_MODEL_V2` never moved.
- **Real-data validation** — validating on real yfinance history (2023–2026, 41
  tickers) is precisely what exposed the tilt as a regime artifact; keep
  real-data, walk-forward validation as the gate.
- **Paper Trader separation** — the preview-only, manual-review trading app stayed
  fully separated from this prediction research; no order, automation, or model
  path was touched.

## What the next alpha research should focus on

The decision JSON's `alpha_rebuild_plan` lays out the backlog. In brief:

1. **Beta/vol-neutralize and re-measure** (cheapest; reuses the 2G/2I harness):
   neutralize forward excess return against beta and realized vol, then re-run the
   IC sweep to see whether any *market-neutral residual* ranking signal survives
   once the risk-premium tilt is removed.
2. **Acquire longer history and a broader universe** — ideally a full market cycle
   including a sustained drawdown, and more than ~40 mega-caps, so a 63d signal can
   be tested out of a single bull regime and the long leg is less concentrated.
3. **Add point-in-time fundamentals / estimate-revision features** — quality,
   value, profitability, analyst revisions, post-earnings drift: signals that are
   not pure market-beta exposure.
4. **Re-run the 2I-A IC sweep + 2I-B robustness battery on each new family.**
5. **Only then**, if a family passes out-of-sample robustness, scope a
   walk-forward model candidate.

**Explicit rule:** no model candidate may be created, and `PREDICTOR_USE_MODEL_V2`
must stay disabled, until a feature family passes out-of-sample robustness
(overlap-aware bootstrap CI excluding zero, permutation significance, and
leave-one-year-out sign stability) on real data spanning more than one market
regime.

## Conclusion

Phase 2I-A found a single measurable signal — a 63d volatility / beta /
correlation tilt — and Phase 2I-B showed it does not survive honest, overlap-aware
robustness testing: every survivor was dropped. The disciplined conclusion is
**NO_GO**: do not build a Phase 2J model candidate and do not promote model-v2 off
a failed risk-premium tilt. Keep the research platform, the safety flags, the
feature-flag discipline, and the real-data validation that made this call
possible, and route the next effort into Phase 2K-A — a new alpha hypothesis
backlog and its data requirements — under the rule that no model candidate is
created until a feature family passes out-of-sample robustness. No **production
edge** is claimed.
